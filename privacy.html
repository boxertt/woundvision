Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Wound Vision is designed as an offline-first clinical documentation and measurement aid. We do not operate a cloud service for the app, and we do not collect, sell, or share personal data from the app.

1. Data you create in the app

The app may store patient records, recording notes, measurement notes, AR video frames, depth data, camera metadata, and exported archive files on your device. This information is created and controlled by the user.

2. Local storage

Data is stored locally on the device unless you choose to export or share it through iOS system sharing features. Deleting app data or deleting the app may remove local records from the device.

3. Export and transfer

Wound Vision supports exporting record packages so users can archive data, move it to a computer, or share it with another clinician for consultation. When you export or share a package, you are responsible for choosing an appropriate secure destination.

4. Permissions

  • Camera and AR permissions are used to record video, depth information, and camera metadata.
  • File access may be used to import or export Wound Vision record packages.
  • Barcode or QR scanning may be used to help fill patient identifiers.

5. No analytics or advertising tracking

The app does not include advertising tracking, third-party analytics, or server-side user profiling.

6. Clinical responsibility

Wound Vision is a documentation and measurement aid. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or automated medical decisions.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact xiazhituo@gmail.com.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 5 月 24 日

创视迹是一个离线优先的临床记录与测量辅助工具。我们不为本应用运营云端服务，也不会从应用中收集、出售或共享个人数据。

1. 你在应用中创建的数据

应用可能在设备本地保存患者档案、录像备注、测量备注、AR 录像帧、深度数据、相机元数据和导出的归档文件。这些信息由用户创建并由用户控制。

2. 本地存储

除非你主动通过 iOS 系统分享功能导出或发送数据，否则数据保存在设备本地。删除应用数据或卸载应用可能会移除设备上的本地记录。

3. 导出与传输

创视迹支持导出记录数据包，便于归档、转移到电脑，或与其他医生进行会诊共享。当你导出或分享数据包时，需要自行选择合适且安全的保存位置或传输方式。

4. 权限使用

  • 相机和 AR 权限用于录制视频、深度信息和相机元数据。
  • 文件访问用于导入或导出创视迹记录数据包。
  • 条形码或二维码扫描用于辅助填写患者标识。

5. 无广告追踪和分析

应用不包含广告追踪、第三方统计分析或服务端用户画像。

6. 临床责任

创视迹是记录和测量辅助工具，不提供诊断、治疗建议或自动化医疗决策。

7. 联系方式

如对本隐私政策有疑问，请联系 xiazhituo@gmail.com。
