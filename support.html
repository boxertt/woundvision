Common workflows

Create a patient

Add the patient name first. Patient ID, hospital, department, and notes can be filled in later.

Record a follow-up

Use the recording screen to capture AR video and depth data. Keep the wound area well lit and avoid rapid camera motion.

Review and measure

Open a recording, choose a frame, and add line, curve, area, or depth-profile measurements with notes.

Before contacting support

  • Tell us the iPhone model and iOS version.
  • Describe the operation that caused the problem.
  • If measurement accuracy is involved, include the expected size and the measured size.
  • Do not send patient-identifiable data by email unless you have permission and a secure workflow.
Wound Vision is a documentation and measurement aid. It does not replace clinical judgment or medical diagnosis.

常用流程

新建患者

优先填写患者姓名。患者 ID、医院、科室和备注可以后续补充或修改。

录制随访

在录制界面采集 AR 录像和深度数据。建议保持创面区域光照充足，避免快速移动镜头。

回看测量

打开录像后选择某一帧，可添加长度、曲线、面积或剖面测量，并补充备注。

联系支持前

  • 请说明 iPhone 型号和 iOS 版本。
  • 请描述问题发生前的操作步骤。
  • 如果是测量精度问题，请提供实际尺寸和测量尺寸。
  • 除非已有授权和安全流程，请不要通过普通邮件发送可识别患者身份的数据。
创视迹是临床记录与测量辅助工具，不能替代医生的诊断和临床判断。
