Clinical wound follow-up

Record the wound, preserve the context.

Wound Vision is an iPhone tool for clinicians to capture AR video, depth information, patient records, notes, and frame-level measurements during wound follow-up.

Designed for clinical follow-up, not casual snapshots.

为临床随访设计，不只是拍一张照片。

Each recording can include patient context, frame metadata, depth maps, camera information, measurements, notes, and exportable archives.

每次录制都可以包含患者信息、逐帧元数据、深度图、相机信息、测量结果、备注，以及可导出的归档数据包。

AR video and depth capture

AR 录像与深度采集

Capture color frames, depth data, camera intrinsics, transforms, and display mapping information for later review and measurement.

记录彩色图像、深度数据、相机内参、位姿变换和显示映射信息，为后续回看和测量保留依据。

Patient and record notes

患者与录像备注

Add notes at the patient, recording, and measurement levels to document wound history and dressing-change progress.

支持在患者、单次录像、单项测量三个层级添加备注，便于记录病因、换药进展和测量说明。

Frame-level measurements

单帧精细测量

Measure line length, curved edges, area, and depth profile on selected frames with visual overlays for review.

在选定帧上进行长度、曲线、面积和剖面测量，并通过可视化标注辅助复核。

Data package first.

以完整数据包为核心。

Wound Vision is built around local control and exportable records, so clinicians can archive cases or transfer them for consultation.

创视迹以本地控制和可导出档案为核心，方便医生归档病例，也方便会诊时传输数据。

Patient archive 患者档案 Name, patient ID, optional hospital and department, patient-level notes, and linked recordings. 包含姓名、患者 ID、可选医院与科室、患者层级备注，以及关联录像。
Recording package 录像数据包 Video frames, metadata, camera information, depth files, ruler calibration, and record notes. 包含视频帧、元数据、相机信息、深度文件、标尺校准和录像备注。
Measurement history 测量历史 Line, curve, area, and profile measurements can be reviewed, named, annotated, and jumped to from the playback timeline. 长度、曲线、面积和剖面测量可回看、命名、备注，并能从回放时间线快速跳转。
Wound Vision is a documentation and measurement aid. It is not intended to replace clinical judgment, diagnosis, or treatment decisions.
创视迹是临床记录与测量辅助工具，不能替代医生的诊断、判断或治疗决策。
